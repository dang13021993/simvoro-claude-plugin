# SimVoro travel eSIM device compatibility

1. Ask for the exact device model and region/variant if not already supplied. Compatibility varies within model families.
2. Use https://simvoro.com/esim/compatible-devices when accessible. Check what is actually listed; otherwise give only general eSIM guidance rather than a guarantee.
3. Explain that the phone usually needs eSIM support, an EID (where applicable), an unlocked carrier status and adequate roaming settings. Certain mainland China / Hong Kong / Macao models may differ. Do not claim a device is compatible from its marketing name alone.
4. Help compare QR-code vs manual installation without asking users to paste activation codes, credentials, IMEI or other sensitive identifiers into chat.
5. Provide a source link and mention that final compatibility must be confirmed before buying. Account-specific support should go through https://simvoro.com/help.
